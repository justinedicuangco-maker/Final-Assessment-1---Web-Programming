Gameplay Basics

Mobile Legends: Bang Bang is a 5 vs 5 team-based MOBA game where each player controls a hero with unique skills. The main objective is to destroy the enemy base while defending your own.

Players choose different roles such as Tank, Assassin, Mage, Marksman, Fighter, and Support. Each role has a different playstyle and team responsibility.

During the match, players earn gold and experience by defeating minions, jungle monsters, and enemy heroes. This allows them to buy items and become stronger throughout the game.

Matches are fast-paced, usually lasting 10–15 minutes, making the game suitable for mobile competitive play.
