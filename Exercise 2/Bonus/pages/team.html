Game Modes

Classic Mode: A casual 5v5 match where players can practice heroes and strategies without ranking pressure.

Ranked Mode: Competitive matches where players climb ranks such as Warrior, Elite, Epic, Legend, and Mythic.

Brawl Mode: A fast-paced mode with random heroes and only one lane for constant action.

VS AI: Practice mode where players fight against computer-controlled enemies.

Custom Mode: Create private matches to play with friends or test strategies.
