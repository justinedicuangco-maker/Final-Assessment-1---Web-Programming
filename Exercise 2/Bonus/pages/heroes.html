Popular Heroes

Alucard - High lifesteal fighter, strong in 1v1 combat and sustain fights.

Miya - Fast marksman with high attack speed and strong late-game damage.

Tigreal - Tank with strong crowd control abilities and team protection skills.

Gusion - Assassin with fast skill combos and high burst damage potential.


Tank Heroes

Tigreal - Strong crowd control tank who can pull and stun multiple enemies.

Grock - Extremely durable tank who can block paths and control space.

Atlas - Can pull and group enemies together for team fight advantages.

Khufra - Counters dash heroes and locks enemies in place with strong control.

Belerick - Reflects damage back to attackers and protects teammates.


Fighter Heroes

Alucard - Lifesteal fighter strong in duels and sustain combat.

Aldous - Gains stacks throughout the game and becomes extremely powerful late game.

Leomord - Mounted fighter with high burst damage and mobility.

Chou - Highly mobile fighter with strong kick-based crowd control.

Thamuz - Sustained damage fighter with strong regeneration abilities.


Assassin Heroes

Fanny - Extremely mobile assassin using cables for fast map movement.

Ling - Agile assassin who can move across walls and deal critical damage.

Gusion - Burst assassin with fast dagger combos and high damage output.

Hayabusa - Ninja assassin with shadow clones and strong burst potential.

Lancelot - High mobility assassin with invincibility frames and burst attacks.


Mage Heroes

Eudora - Simple burst mage with strong stun and electric damage.

Kagura - Advanced mage with umbrella control and high skill ceiling.

Lunox - Dual-form mage using light and dark powers.

Valir - Fire mage with knockback and area damage abilities.

Pharsa - Long-range mage with powerful ultimate bombardment.


Marksman Heroes

Layla - Beginner-friendly marksman with long attack range.

Miya - Fast attacking marksman with stealth ultimate ability.

Granger - Burst damage marksman using gun-based attacks.

Claude - Mobile marksman with clone attacks and fast movement.

Beatrix - Versatile marksman who can switch between multiple weapons.


Support Heroes

Rafaela - Healer support that increases team movement speed.

Angela - Can attach to allies and support them anywhere on the map.

Estes - Strong healing support for team sustain in fights.

Diggie - Counters crowd control and protects teammates.

Floryn - Global healer who can support allies across the map.
