Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft, chewy chocolate chip cookies with crispy edges and a gooey center. Perfect for snacks, parties, or dessert cravings.

Ingredients

  • 125g butter (softened)
  • 100g brown sugar
  • 50g white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 150g chocolate chips
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C and line a baking tray.
  2. Cream butter and sugars until smooth.
  3. Add egg and vanilla, mix well.
  4. Add flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Stir in chocolate chips.
  6. Roll into small balls and place on tray.
  7. Bake for 10–12 minutes.
  8. Let cool and enjoy.
